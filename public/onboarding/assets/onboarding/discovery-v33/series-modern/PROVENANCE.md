Fetched read-only from https://tryboks.com on 2026-11-16. All images, narration and text/timing data are authentic current Boks production assets. No images, voice or literary text were generated for this bundle.

`index.json` records public source URLs, SHA-256 hashes, dimensions, story IDs and the production registry dates. `source-manifest.json` is byte-exact; `manifest.json` changes only asset URLs to local relative paths. All palette, safe zones, phrase and emphasis markers, timing settings, words and token timestamps are preserved.

Do not claim either is uniquely the most recently uploaded work.
