`work/boks-design-atlas/screen-index.json` to real application code. These are
states, not routes. No fixtures or prototype router were imported.

`code` means inspected implementation, `code+unit` includes focused automated
coverage, `code+QA-report` includes a recorded local browser check, `partial`
identifies a deliberately unsupported data promise, `intent` is an unproduced
content example, and `preserved` retains authoritative existing copy. These
labels never imply that all devices or every error branch were exercised.
QA rows refer to actual local browser actions, or explicitly label a product
adaptation/content boundary. Destructive confirmation previews do not imply
execution. All 35 Studio states have a rendered record. Exact local screenshot
inventories are in `work/v44-qa/pass2-reader.md`, `pass2-formats.md`,
`pass2-social.md`, and `pass2-admin.md` (ignored work folder). Final validation
and remaining device checks are recorded in
`docs/handover/2026-10-17-design-v44-implementation.md`.
