# Edgar Allan Poe — "Spirits of the Dead"

The 1829 text from *Al Aaraaf, Tamerlane and Minor Poems* (Wikisource). First of
the Halloween three, chosen to catch the eye.

## Image order

1. `01_alone_by_the_tombstone` — alone by the grey tombstone (stanza 1).
2. `02_spirits_around_the_candle` — the spirits of the dead around him (stanza 2).
3. `03_red_orbs` — the night that frowns, the stars as red orbs (stanzas 3–4).
4. `04_mist_upon_the_hill` — the mist upon the hill (stanza 5).

## Prompt

Build a four-scene vertical immersive experience for Edgar Allan Poe's "Spirits
of the Dead", using spirits_of_the_dead_01–04 in order and synchronizing each
transition with the corresponding stanza. During the second scene, let the
listener press and hold the candle: its flame becomes steadier, the fog moves,
and the surrounding spirits gradually become visible; releasing it returns them
to the mist. Keep the interaction optional, use slow parallax and 600–900 ms
cinematic dissolves, and never pause the narration.
