# Emily Dickinson — "One need not be a chamber to be haunted"

The 1891 text (*Poems, Second Series*, printed there as "Ghosts"), not the
later manuscript edition. Second of the Halloween three: the unusual gesture.

## Image order

1. `01_the_chamber` — the bedroom and the house (stanza 1).
2. `02_corridors_of_the_mind` — the house becomes corridors of the mind (stanzas 2–3).
3. `03_the_mirror` — "Ourself, behind ourself concealed" (stanza 4).
4. `04_the_bolted_door` — the prudent bolts the door (stanza 5).

## Prompt

Build a four-scene psychological immersive experience for Emily Dickinson's
"One Need Not Be a Chamber — to Be Haunted", using one_need_not_be_a_chamber_01–04
in order as the house transforms into the corridors of the protagonist's mind.
In the mirror scene, let the listener wipe condensation from the glass with a
finger, gradually revealing a reflection that stands one step behind the real
character; the reflection should remain visible after the finger is lifted. Use
subtle spatial audio, candle flicker and restrained transitions without jump
scares or blocking the narration.
