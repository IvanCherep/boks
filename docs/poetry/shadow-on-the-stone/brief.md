# Thomas Hardy — "The Shadow on the Stone"

From *Moments of Vision* (1917; Project Gutenberg #3255). Last of the Halloween
three, to end on something human.

## Image order

1. `01_the_druid_stone` — the Druid stone and the shifting shadows (lines 1–4).
2. `02_her_shadow` — the shadows shape into her head and shoulders (lines 5–8).
3. `03_behind_his_back` — "I would not turn my head" (stanza 2 and lines 17–20).
4. `04_leaving_the_glade` — he goes on softly and leaves her shadow (lines 21–24).

## Prompt

Build a four-scene elegiac ghost story for Thomas Hardy's "The Shadow on the
Stone", using the_shadow_on_the_stone_01–04 in order and letting the woman's
shadow emerge naturally from moving branches. Allow the listener to drag
horizontally to make the protagonist begin turning around: the farther the drag
moves, the more the apparition disappears, while releasing before completion
restores it. Keep the action optional and emotionally quiet, with falling
leaves, shifting sunlight and slow transitions synchronized to the narration.
