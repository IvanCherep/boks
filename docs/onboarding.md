# Reader introduction (onboarding)

The designers' introduction (prototype v54, handover
`2026-11-19-onboarding-implementation.md`) meets a new guest over the reader:
welcome room → reading languages → form → classics shelf → Series painting →
mystery mail → afisha → final invitation, landing on the feed's real first bok.

## Who sees it

`lib/boks/onboarding/eligibility.ts`: nobody is sent into it. A guest with no
record in `localStorage["boks-onboarding"]` sees "Знакомство с boks" on the
cover, above "Что почитать", and opens it when they want to; finishing or
skipping writes the record and the invitation goes. Accounts never see the
invitation. `/?welcome=1` opens it on purpose, for anyone.

## How it is built

- `public/onboarding/` is generated by `node scripts/onboarding/import.mjs
  <prototype>/dist`; never edit it by hand. The import copies only what the
  scenes use and keeps living authors' files out (their Series come from
  link-only folders, portraits from `/a/<handle>/photo`). It also patches the
  demo endings to `scripts/onboarding/bridge.js`, re-encodes pictures (WebP q72,
  book cloth at 600 px; classics' portraits untouched), and bundles the
  scripts and styles into one minified file each.
- `components/boks/onboarding/onboarding-frame.tsx` shows the page in a
  same-origin frame (`framedBySelf` in `lib/boks/security-headers.ts`).
  The reader keeps loading underneath and holds its reading clock.
- Bridge messages: `boks-onboarding-answers` (after the shelf), `-done`,
  `-sign-in`; boks sends `boks-first-book` so the final scene shows the bok the
  reader will land on.

## The answers

`reader_starting_preferences` (migration `20261121100000`), written by
`save_my_starting_preferences` for the current (also anonymous) user: status,
form, reading languages, chosen classics (ids and ru/en names), visitor id.
Signing in from the final scene carries the guest's answers into the account
(`p_adopt`), unless the account already has its own. Reading languages also
become the reader's reading languages.

Ranking (`lib/boks/onboarding/starting-preferences.ts`, applied in
`serveReaderFeed`): a chosen classic lifts matching boks 6 places, the
preferred form 3, fading to nothing over the reader's first 40 reading events.
Lifted items carry `starting:classic` / `starting:form-<form>` in their stored
explanations (`served_feed_item.explanations`).

## Weight (slow phone: CPU ×4, 1.6 Mbit/s, 150 ms)

- Prototype as supplied: first room usable after 13–17 s, 3.3 MB before it.
- Here: ~660 KB before the first room (brotli), usable ~4 s standalone and
  ~10 s from opening boks (the reader loads alongside). Scene changes keep a
  16.7 ms median frame; single long tasks ≤150 ms at scene boundaries.
